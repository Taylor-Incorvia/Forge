# Blink Zealot one-base all-in — watch, don't nerf (yet)

## What
Observation, not a confirmed problem. Taylor lost to a Blink Zealot one-base all-in over the last few days and it *felt* strong. **No change made — gathering more data before acting.**

## Why it's not a nerf signal yet
The loss is **confounded by a greedy opening**: Taylor opened 2 Barracks and expanded, then got hit by more Zealots than that build was ready for. A greedy eco opening losing to a one-base all-in is true in *standard* SC2 too — that's the risk/reward of teching/expanding vs. staying safe. One greedy loss ≠ imbalance.

Scan is *not* the lever here (already reasoned through): walling doesn't stop Blink Zealots (walk up the ramp, blink the wall), so gating/removing Scan wouldn't fix it and is inelegant. Ghost Academy price has been eyeballed for months without pulling the trigger — that hesitation is itself a signal to leave it.

## The signal to watch
- Taylor (or others) try Blink Zealot 1-base all-in **on purpose, from an even game** (not while already behind) and it wins with little counterplay → real signal, act.
- It only wins vs greedy/behind players → working as intended, leave it.

Cheapest possible version to stress-test: mine ~225 gas for Blink, then all minerals, show up and blink around the base. If *that* reliably wins from even, it's oppressive.

## Action spectrum (only if confirmed, in order)
1. Nudge Ghost Academy (blink research facility) cost/time up so Blink comes online later.
2. Re-look at Zealot count/cost scaling on one base.
3. Only then consider Blink availability timing.

## Notes
Related: the Tempest "oppressive" games were also confounded by Taylor being behind first (failed early attack → opponent over-teched). Pattern across this review session: "felt OP" games were mostly "I was already behind" games. Balance on confirmed even-game data, not on feels-while-losing.
