# Upgrade ideas inbox (low priority — new functionality, maybe never)

A holding pen for "would be cool, not committed" upgrade ideas so they're captured without spawning
a formal ticket each. Taylor's stance (2026-09-07): **all new functionality is low priority** — he's
guarding his mental headspace and has accepted he won't do everything. Promote an item to its own ticket
only when he actually decides to build it. Nothing here is scheduled.

## Ideas

### 1. Marauder second concussive variant — AoE slow (seen in Patches mod)
A second Marauder upgrade (gated behind / building on Concussive Shells) where the Marauder's hit applies
a **slow in an AoE around the target**, not just the target. Fits the existing concussive family
([[WA-105]], [[WA-063]]). Legible if the AoE has an obvious visual ([[balance-upgrade-legibility]]).

### 2. Ultralisk lifesteal (Patches has 10% → try 20% in Wildcard)
Lifesteal upgrade for the Ultralisk, ~**20%** (Patches uses 10%; Wildcard tends to push numbers). Mirror
the existing `LifestealMarine` wiring.

### 3. Colossus "Scorching Flame" DoT (from a Patches/other showcase screenshot)
Colossus upgrade: attacks deal an **additional ~15 damage over ~3.15s, NON-stacking**. Because it doesn't
stack, splitting Colossus fire across more targets applies it more widely — **rewards micro**. Reference
values from the screenshot: cost 150/150, research 90s, Robotics Bay. Adapt cost/time to Wildcard's
research engine ([[WA-092]]) if built. Legibility: needs an obvious burning visual on afflicted targets.
(Loosely related to the "Colossus fire-trail" Season-2 idea noted in [[WA-078]].)
