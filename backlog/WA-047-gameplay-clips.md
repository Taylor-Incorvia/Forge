# Play the game + capture gameplay clips (Reddit credibility)

Build a small library of short gameplay clips. **Every Reddit post needs an attached clip** — a text-only post reads like advertising a *separate* game, not an SC2 mod. A clip instantly proves it: recognizable SC2 units/UI/engine, doing the wildcard thing.

## Why
The clip is the credibility proof *and* the hook in one. Seeing a Marine that blinks, a Stalker that stims, or cloaked Dusk Wings massed — in the unmistakable SC2 client — tells a skeptical redditor "this is a real SC2 mod, and it's doing something I've never seen." No clip, no trust.

## What makes a good clip
- **Recognizable unit doing something impossible in standard SC2** (the wildcard hook): stimming Stalker, blinking Marine, Ultralisk that rolled armor, MissilePods off a Phoenix, cloaked Dusk Wings, Corrosive Bile from a Marauder, etc.
- **"Adapt to your hand" swings** — read a bad roll, tech to the counter your buildings gave you, win the fight.
- **Short + punchy** (~10-30s), readable, minimal UI clutter. Ideally show the *roll* then the *payoff*.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Capture setup working (OBS, or SC2 replay + capture — replays let you re-shoot the same moment from better angles/zoom).
- [ ] Gather an initial batch (~5-10 clips) covering varied rolls/units/moments — not five clips of the same thing.
- [ ] Store + label them (folder + one line per clip: what it shows, which post idea it fits).
- [ ] Going forward: every Reddit post has a matching clip ready.

## Notes
Ongoing/recurring content task, not one-and-done — feeds Phase 4 (Marketing) and the funnel (YouTube → Website → Discord → Queue). **Save replays** of good games; you can mine multiple clips/angles from one replay later. Parallel track — not gated by Phase 1 game-readiness work.
