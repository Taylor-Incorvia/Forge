## Reddit (r/starcraft)

**Title:** Wildcard Arena Season 1 is keeping the 12-worker start (and the 400 mineral CC). Here's the thinking.

**Body:**

Quick context if you haven't seen it: **Wildcard Arena** is a 1v1–4v4 custom mod where every game your Barracks/Factory/Starport roll a semi-random mix of units from all three races, and your tech buildings roll random upgrades. You macro like normal SC2 — you just adapt to the hand you're dealt.

On the economy: I built and balanced the entire mod on the **12-worker / 400-CC** economy, and it feels good. So **Season 1 is 12 workers.** I'll watch where the ladder economy lands and reconsider *if* it settles into something coherent — but I'm not chasing weekly changes mid-season.

Reasons beyond "most of you seem to prefer it":

- Every balance decision I've made (unit costs, proxy timings, all of it) was tested on 12/400. Switching mid-stream quietly invalidates months of tuning.
- The one that's specific to a random-unit game: **you can't reliably all-in in Wildcard** — you attack with whatever you rolled, so there's no guaranteed timing push. That already favors the greedy macro player, and a cheaper Command Center tilts it *further*. A mode like this has *more* reason to keep the expensive CC than the ladder does.

Short version: if you miss the 12-worker economy, Wildcard Arena is a place you can still play it.

Genuinely curious where people land — is the 8-worker start growing on anyone, or still universally hated? [Discord: LINK]
