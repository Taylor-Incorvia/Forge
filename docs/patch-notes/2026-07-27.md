**Wildcard Arena — Patch Notes**
Casters get a real reason to tech, a round of cost tuning, and some quality-of-life. (Rename this file to the actual publish date.)

**Casters Regenerate Energy Faster**
- When a caster researches its rolled spell, it now also gains **+25% energy regeneration** — for the rest of the game.
- This rewards teching into your casters: more casts over a game, without letting you dump a huge burst of casts in a single fight.
- Applies to every caster that can roll a spell — pure casters (Sentry, Medic, Infestor, High Templar, Raven, Viper, Science Vessel) and the fighter-casters (Queen, Wraith, Phoenix, Corsair, Ghost) when they roll a spell.

**Balance — Costs**
- **Ultralisk:** mineral cost increased (now 325 minerals). It was overperforming as a mass unit.
- **Thor:** gas cost reduced (now 300/150) — it was a bit too weak for the investment.
- **War Hound:** re-costed to 200/75.
- **Raven, Viper:** mineral cost reduced 100 → **75** (they were under-used; still gas-gated).
- **Infestor:** mineral cost reduced 100 → **50**.
- **Corsair:** energy normalized to standard (200 max, standard regen) so the new caster-regen buff applies evenly.

**Balance — Production & Units**
- **Stalker:** builds noticeably faster from the Factory (build time 25s → 18s). A build-time buff, not a cost cut — Stalkers were under-produced when you committed to them.
- **Hydralisk:** no longer starts with Grooved Spines (range 6 → 5). It already out-trades its cost in raw stats; range is now something it can *roll* instead of getting for free.

**Removed / Pool Changes**
- **Stim** removed from Zerglings (it did effectively nothing on them).
- **Twin-Linked Flamethrowers** removed from the Hellion pool — a near-invisible dud. A proper "flame wall" upgrade is planned to replace it.

**Quality of Life**
- Picked Zerg or Protoss? Your workers now **auto-mine at 0:00** like a Terran start — no more hand-ordering them onto minerals.
- The **Queen** is now included in **Select All Army**.
