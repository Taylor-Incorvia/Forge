**Wildcard Arena 3.0**
The big one: a "Your Faction" panel so you can actually see the hand you were dealt. Plus a full caster overhaul, a round of cost tuning, and quality-of-life.

**NEW — The "Your Faction" Panel**
- At the start of every game, a panel shows **every unit you rolled** with its **rolled upgrade paired directly beneath it**, grouped by production facility.
- **Hover any icon** for its full description — the exact tooltip you'd see on the command card.
- A **FACTION tab** on the left edge of the screen opens and closes it anytime.
- No more squinting at a chat message to remember what you rolled — your whole faction is one glance away.

**Casters Overhauled**
- **+25% energy regeneration** when a caster researches its rolled spell — for the rest of the game. Teching into a caster now means *more casts over the whole game*, rather than one hyper-efficient spell you dump in a single fight.
- With regen doing that job, **spell energy costs moved back toward standard**: Force Field / Graviton Beam / Auto-Turret 40 → 50, Guardian Shield / Fungal Growth 50 → 75, Neural Parasite 75 → 100, Parasitic Bomb 100 → 125, Abduct 50 → 75, Irradiate 40 → 50.
- **Corsair** energy normalized to standard (200 max) so the new regen applies evenly.

**Balance — Costs**
- **Ultralisk:** mineral cost increased to **325** — it was overperforming as a mass unit.
- **Thor:** gas cost reduced to **300/150** — too weak for the investment.
- **War Hound:** re-costed to **200/75**.
- **Raven, Viper:** minerals **100 → 75**; **Infestor:** minerals **100 → 50**. They were under-used, and they're still gas-gated.

**Balance — Units**
- **Stalker:** builds noticeably faster from the Factory (**25s → 18s**). A build-time buff, not a cost cut — Stalkers were under-produced when you committed to them.
- **Hydralisk:** no longer starts with Grooved Spines (range 6 → 5). It already out-trades its cost on raw stats; range is now something it can *roll* instead of getting free.

**Removed / Pool Changes**
- **Stim** removed from Zerglings (did effectively nothing on them).
- **Twin-Linked Flamethrowers** removed from the Hellion pool — a near-invisible dud. A proper "flame wall" upgrade is planned to replace it.
- **Blink** no longer rolls on the **Stalker** — it already starts with Blink, so it was rolling a redundant second one.

**Quality of Life**
- Picked Zerg or Protoss? Your workers now **auto-mine at 0:00** like a Terran start — no more hand-ordering them onto minerals.
- The **Queen** is now included in **Select All Army**.

**Bug Fixes**
- **Disruption Web** tooltip now correctly states it stops **all** ground units and structures under it (yours and the enemy's) — it's area denial, so cast it where the enemy is and keep your own units clear.
