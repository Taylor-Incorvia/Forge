**Wildcard Arena v0.7.3 — 2026-09-13**
A clarity + fairness pass, plus a few unit tweaks. Nothing reworked — just sanding down rough edges.

**Stalker — no longer starts with Blink**
- Blink used to be free on the Stalker from the moment it rolled. Now it's just one of the upgrades a Stalker can roll, like any other unit. No more Blink Stalkers as your very first unit, and because it's one upgrade per slot, you can't stack Blink + Speed on the same Stalker anymore.
- The experimental "enhanced Blink" upgrades (longer-range / shorter-cooldown Blink) are gone — they were trying to cram two upgrades into a one-upgrade system. That idea is shelved for later.

**Raven — minerals 75 → 100 (back to standard)**
- The discount was meant to reward micro-heavy caster play but it didn't — mass Raven + Seeker Missile just played as a-click and got oppressive in volume. Removing an unjustified discount.

**Lurker — range upgrade buffed (+2 → +3)**
- Base range 8 → **11**, and the spike count grows to match so the impale visibly reaches farther. Ladder's version is only +2; the Wildcard roll should feel like a real payoff.

**Vulture — cleanup**
- Removed the leftover KD8 Charge (reaper grenade) button that showed on the Vulture's card but did nothing.

**Readability — a big tooltip fix**
- Fixed a large batch of upgrade and ability names that were showing their raw internal id (things like "GoliathRange2" or "Range2") instead of a readable name. Every research now shows a proper name and description when you hover it — on the command card and in the Your Faction panel.
