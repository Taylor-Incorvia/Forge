**Wildcard Arena — Season 1 patch (WIP)**

**Bug Fixes**
- **Scout icons now require active vision.** The row of icons above an enemy production/tech structure shows **only while you currently have vision of that building**, and disappears the moment you lose sight of it. Previously the icons could **linger** over a structure you'd merely explored the ground near but weren't actively watching — so a single early scout could reveal a base's rolls (even buildings that went up *later*) without you keeping eyes on it. Scouting is now a live, ongoing cost, the way it was always meant to be — see an enemy building, see its rolls; leave, and the info goes dark.
