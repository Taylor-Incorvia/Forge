**Wildcard Arena v0.7.2 — 2026-08-31**
Research is no longer one-size-fits-all. Until now every upgrade cost 150/150 and took 87s to research; now cost and research time reflect how strong an upgrade is.

**Research — Cheaper / Faster**
- **Concussive Shells:** **50/50, ~43s** (was 150/150, ~87s). research cost and build time match ladder
- **Force Field:** **100/100, ~50s** (was 150/150, ~87s). Force fields aren't useful once your opponent has massive units. Needed to get out faster to be useful. 

**Research — More Expensive / Slower**
- **Blink (Barracks slot-1 units):** research time up to **~137s** (was ~87s) — It was absurd when zealots/zerglings that can hop walls, escape surrounds, jump on workers, etc would show up in your base before the 3 minute mark. 
- **Yamato Cannon — now scales with the unit:**
  - On a **Battlecruiser**: **150/150, 100s**.
  - On **anything else** (Thor, Ultralisk, Colossus): **200/200, ~120s**. Yamato is very strong on units that are cheaper than BCs, so it now costs accordingly.

Every other upgrade still researches at the standard **150/150, ~87s**.
